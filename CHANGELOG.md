## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-12

- `create`: create a reservation (baseline).
- `list-mine`: list my reservations, optional status filter (LIST-01 to LIST-04).
- `cancel`: cancel a reservation with at least 2 hours notice (REQ-01 to REQ-04).
